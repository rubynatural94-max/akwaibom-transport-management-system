Case Study Background

Akwa Ibom State Transport

The Akwa Ibom Transport Company (AKTC) is a state-owned transport operator headquartered in Uyo, established in 1988 shortly after the creation of Akwa Ibom State — originally to convey civil servants around the state, later expanding into inter-state passenger and cargo transport.

As operations grew, the manual, paper-based approach to tracking staff records, vehicle status, and route information became harder to manage — records get lost, updates are slow, and there's no quick way to check who's on duty or which buses are due for maintenance. This project proposes a lightweight, browser-based system to digitize those records.

System Modules

What this system manages

Four connected modules, all backed by a shared staff-login system so every action can be traced back to who did it.

01

Staff Records

Add, update, search and remove staff/driver records — role, department, position, and employment status.

02

Bus Fleet

Track every vehicle by plate number, capacity, assigned driver, and current status.

03

Routes & Fares

Maintain route origins, destinations, distances, and current fares for every corridor served.

04

Login Activity

Every sign-in is logged with a timestamp, so admins can see who accessed the system and when.

05

Role-based Access

Staff and admin accounts see the same interface, with admin-only actions clearly separated.

06

Live Dashboard

An at-a-glance overview of total staff, active buses, and routes the moment you sign in.
